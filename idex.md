# Privacy Policy for ReMesh

**Last updated:** November 3, 2026

This privacy policy governs your use of the software application **ReMesh** ("Application") for mobile devices, created and maintained by **Štěpán Remeš** ("Developer", "we", "us"). 

The Application provides users with the ability to scan NFC tags, share profile information, and interact with other users. We are committed to protecting and respecting your privacy.

---

## 1. Information Collection and Use

### Mandatory Information
To create an account and use the core functionalities of the Application, you must provide:
*   **First Name and Last Name**
*   **Email Address**

### Optional Profile Information
You may choose to provide additional, non-mandatory information to complete your user profile:
*   **Phone Number**
*   **Gender**
*   **Social Media Links** (Facebook, Instagram)

### Location Data (NFC Scanning)
The Application requests access to your device's location services. However, **we do not track your device's continuous location**. 
*   Your location is accessed **temporarily and only at the precise moment you scan an NFC tag**.
*   This location data is processed to determine and save the geographical "location of the tag" itself. 
*   We do not use or store your personal device location background data outside of this specific action.

---

## 2. Profile Visibility and User Interactions

### Profile Privacy Settings
The Application features user profiles. **You have full control over what information is visible to other users.** 
*   By default, you can choose to hide or display any of your optional data (phone number, gender, social media links) or mandatory data on your public profile.
*   Your profile can be configured to show everything you entered, or nothing at all to the public.

### Anonymous User Contact
After an NFC tag is scanned, other users may initiate contact with you through the Application. **By default, this initial contact mechanism is strictly anonymous** to protect your identity and personal contact details unless you choose to explicitly reveal them.

## 3. Third-Party Services and Analytics

To help us understand how the Application is used and to improve the user experience, we utilize third-party developer tools. Specifically, this Application uses:

*   **Google Firebase (Firebase Analytics):** This service helps us collect anonymized technical data, such as app crashes, device models, operating system versions, and basic usage statistics. Google Firebase processes this data in accordance with their own privacy policy, which can be found at: [https://google.com](https://google.com).

We will retain your personal information only for as long as your account is active. If you wish to delete your account and all associated personal data from our servers, you can do so directly within the Application settings or by contacting the Developer at the email address provided below.
