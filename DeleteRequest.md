## ReMeSH – Account Deletion

If you wish to delete your ReMeSH account and associated personal data, you can do so directly in the app under Account Settings.

If you no longer have access to the app, you can request account deletion by sending an email to our contact address. Please include the email address associated with your account. We will verify your identity before proceeding with the deletion.

Deleting your account will permanently remove your personal information and associated data in accordance with our Privacy Policy.
